Computer Vision · Visual Representation Learning · Agricultural Phenotyping

I am a Ph.D. student at Tottori University working on computer vision and image processing. My research focuses on extracting plant traits from RGB images and developing visual recognition methods that incorporate domain-specific knowledge.

GitHub

Research

Research Interests

My research explores computer vision methods for understanding plant morphology and phenotypic traits from visual observations.

01

Image-based Plant Phenotyping

Estimating plant traits from RGB images with an emphasis on non-destructive and practical phenotyping in field environments.

02

Domain Knowledge for Visual Recognition

Incorporating domain-specific structural knowledge into visual recognition while reducing the need for dense manual annotations.

03

Visual Representation Learning

Investigating how representations from visual foundation models can be adapted to regression and fine-grained prediction tasks.
